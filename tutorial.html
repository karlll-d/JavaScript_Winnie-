FRUIT OF THE SPIRIT

As stated in Galatians 5:22-23, the fruit of the spirit is love, joy, peace, forbearance, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness and self-control. Now let’s dive into each aspect of fruit of the spirit in details.

Love

When we say love, what comes into mind? Well, a lot of people have their own definition for love. For instance, my roommate defines love as putting others first before considering yourself which aligns with Philippians 2:4: “do not merely look out for your own interests, but also for the interests of others”. I know some might disagree with this because of Galatians 6:5 where Apostle Paul said “for each one should carry their own load. However when you read the scriptures in a proper context, using that verse to justify a selfish mindset misinterprets its true meaning. The bible explicitly state in 1 John 4:8 that “whoever does not love does not God because God is love”. So now what is love? Love is simply God because when we talk about the attribute of God, it stated that God is love. We can get the real meaning of Love through the actions of God. Consider John 3:16 “for God so loved the world that he gave his only begotten son that whoever believe in him will not perish but have everlasting life”. We hear this verse often but pay close attention to this verse. What did you deduce from it? You’ll agree with me that love is sacrificial. Out of His great love for us, he had to sacrifice his only son for our sins. You claim you love your brother or your sister or friends who is lost in Christ but have you ever taken the pain to sacrifice your time to share the gospel with them so they will turn to Christ. Love is demonstrated by giving up something to benefit others even when they don’t deserve it. The big question, why must you love your enemy? Romans 5:10 reminds us that we were once God’s enemies but we reconciled with him through the death of his son. This was all because of the love he had for us and since we were created in His image we must emulate His ways. Truth be told, we are humans so loving your enemy can be challenging but 1 John 4:20 clearly state that “whoever claims to love God yet hates a brother or sister is a liar. For whoever does not love their brother or sister, whom they have seen, cannot love God, whom they have not seen.” Fortunately for us we have the Holy Spirit. Loving your enemies is achieved by the in dwelling of the Holy Spirit. When the Holy Spirit is in you, he convicts you, he fills you with love, isn’t that such a wonderful thing. Ultimately, love is the greatest commandment therefore we ought to love others just as God loves us.
